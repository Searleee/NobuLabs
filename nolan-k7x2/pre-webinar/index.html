Why your willpower runs out by Wednesday

Preview It was never a character flaw

Hey {FIRST NAME},

Most men think discipline is something you either have or you don't.

So every Monday they decide to have it. New routine, new alarm, new rules. And by Wednesday it's gone, and they take that as proof they're just not a disciplined person.

It isn't proof of anything.

Willpower is a fight you have to win again every single morning, against a phone that was designed by very smart people to beat you. You were always going to lose that eventually.

There's an older idea that works better. Sun Tzu wrote that the victorious warrior wins first and then goes to war, while the one who loses goes to war and then hopes to win.

Turned inward, it means you don't win at the moment of temptation. You win the night before, in how you set up your room, your phone and your morning.

I call it Win-First Structure, and on Sunday I'll show you exactly how I build it.
