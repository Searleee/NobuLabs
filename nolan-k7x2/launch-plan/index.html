Six phases · three weeks

The launch playbook

01 · Build-upMon 16 to Sun 22 Nov · Nolan posts, we script it
The anchor reel

Most plans die around day 18. Here's why.

Hook on screen DAY 18

Stories

A poll every day: "Have you ever abandoned a plan by week three?" Then a question box: "What made you stop?" We screenshot the answers for the reveal.

Feed

The build-up carousel (in this pitch) on Tuesday, the day-18 reel on Thursday. Both end on the same line: something is coming on the 29th.

Us

We write every story frame and caption, and pull the best poll answers into the session slides.

Goal

Get as many men as possible to say out loud that they have this problem.

02 · InvitationMon 23 to Wed 25 Nov · Nolan posts, Adam answers the DMs
The invitation post

I'm teaching the exact setup I run around a full-time job, live and free on Sunday.

Hook on screen COMMENT LOCK IN

Feed

The invitation post (in this pitch). Every comment of LOCK IN gets an automatic DM with the registration link.

Email

An early-access email to the Structure Starter list (about 2,500 men) goes out first, on Monday morning.

WhatsApp

Registrants land in the group. The Win-First Night guide is pinned as the first message.

Goal

Fill the WhatsApp group before Sunday. Every registrant is a warm lead.

03 · AnticipationThu 26 to Sat 28 Nov · inside the group, run by us
The group warm-up

Before Sunday, do your Life Audit. We build on it live.

Hook on screen HOMEWORK BEFORE SUNDAY

Day one

A two-minute voice note from Nolan: why he's doing this, and what to bring on Sunday.

Day two

A question to the group: "What's the one thing you'd do every day if you knew you wouldn't quit?" Nolan replies to a few.

Day three

The timeline, crystal clear: Sunday 7pm Eastern, live link at 6:55, and "I'm opening something up at the end."

Goal

Make Sunday feel like an event the men have already invested in.

04 · RevealSun 29 Nov, 7pm Eastern · Nolan live, we produce
The Lock-In Session

How young men are locking in for 30 days straight by deciding the fight before it starts.

Hook on screen LIVE NOW

Live

The session presentation (in this pitch): the three pillars, then Living Telos, $97, doors close Thursday.

Group

The checkout link goes into the WhatsApp group and the live chat at the same moment.

Bonus

Anyone who joins before the session ends gets the Personal Code Workbook.

Goal

Open the doors to a room that already trusts him.

05 · ConversionMon 30 Nov to Wed 2 Dec · Adam on the DMs
One-to-one follow-up

Saw you were on Sunday. Any questions about the cohort?

Hook on screen DOORS ARE OPEN

Group

A poll the morning after: "I'm in", "I have a question", "Not this time". Everyone who picks the middle option gets a DM.

DMs

Adam replies to every raised hand from Nolan's account, using an approved answer bank for the common questions: time, the job, the refund.

Proof

Screenshots of men posting "I'm in" go back into the group and stories.

Goal

Most of the sales happen here, one conversation at a time.

06 · UrgencyThu 3 Dec · countdown in the group and stories
The real close

Doors close at midnight. The founding price goes with them.

Hook on screen 24 HOURS LEFT

Countdown

Messages at 24, 12, 6 and 2 hours, then "closed". Each one names what they miss: the cohort starting Monday, the founding price, the live calls.

Email

A last-chance email to every registrant who hasn't bought.

Rule

The close is real. No reopening. That's what makes the next launch work.

Goal

Turn the men who were "going to do it later" into founding members.
