03 · The problem · Day 18Sign-up +1 day · 3 of 8
Subject

Why most plans die around day 18

Preview It isn't discipline

Hey {FIRST NAME},

Watch what happens to almost any plan a man makes.

The first week is easy, because it's new. The second week runs on momentum. Then somewhere in the third week the novelty is gone, nobody's watching, and one missed day starts to feel like failing.

So he stops, and he tells himself he isn't a disciplined person.

He's wrong about the reason. Motivation was always going to run out around then. What he needed was a reason that held when it did, a setup that made starting easy, and someone who'd notice when he went quiet.

Those are the three parts of Sunday.

Nolan

What an acorn knows that you don't

Preview One Greek word that explains your drifting

Hey {FIRST NAME},

Aristotle had a word for the end a thing is for. Telos.

An acorn's telos is the oak. It doesn't need motivation to grow. It has a direction built in.

Most of us don't. We have goals, which is different. "Get my life together" is a wish. "In a year I'm the man my younger brother copies" is a direction you can check yourself against on a tired Tuesday.

On Sunday I'll take you through the three questions I use to write mine. Ten minutes, and you'll have it on paper.
