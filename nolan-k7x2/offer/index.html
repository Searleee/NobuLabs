Before you apply

The four questions every man asks

Will it work for me?

It starts by choosing, not adding

The masterclass shows you how to win the day before it starts. Becoming Whole decides which battles are worth winning. Instead of one more demanding routine on a crowded life, you pick three priorities and let the rest wait.

Can I keep it up?

It plans for your bad weeks

Intense routines collapse the first time life gets busy. Your minimum viable week is the floor you hold on a hard week, so you keep your momentum instead of starting over.

Why Nolan?

He rebuilt himself this way at 20

After boxing, he put his life back together with philosophy, a journal and a structured day. He turns those principles into real decisions about career, fitness and study, and he works through every session with you himself.

Why now?

Ten founding places

This is the first intake, and every session is one-on-one with Nolan, so it's capped at ten. Once the method is proven it moves to recorded lessons and group coaching. The founding ten are the only men who get him one-on-one.
