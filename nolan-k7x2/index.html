03

The plan

Start with ten men. Prove it. Then scale it.

You don't have a paid offer yet, so we don't launch a big one. The first intake is ten founding places, one-on-one with you for five weeks. That proves the method works and gives you ten real stories to show. Once you have them, the same method moves to recorded lessons and a live weekly group call, so it can take far more men without taking far more of your time. 122K people saved one focus protocol of yours. Ten founding men out of that audience isn't a stretch.

04

The objection you're already forming

"That sounds like a lot more work." For five weeks, it is.

The founding intake is real work: one session a week with each of the ten men, plus one group call, for five weeks. That's the point. It's how you prove the method and earn the stories that sell the next intake. After that the lessons are recorded and coaching runs as one live group call a week. And there's no sales call at any stage. Men apply, you pick who's in, and the ones you pick get an email with a payment link.

  • 10 × 1:1sessions a week, for the five founding weeks only
  • 1 group calla week with the founding ten, and the format you scale on
  • 0sales calls. Men apply, you choose the ten.
